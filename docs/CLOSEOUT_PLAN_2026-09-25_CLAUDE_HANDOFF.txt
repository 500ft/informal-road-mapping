Exact specification:
1. A full history is four bits in year order 2023,2024,2025,2026. Enumerate 16.
2. A retention mask is four bits; include all with 2, 3 or 4 retained years:
   C(4,2)+C(4,3)+C(4,4)=11 masks. Enumerate 176 history/mask pairs, including
   unchanged all-year controls. Some pairs share an observed history; these
   are enumerated scenarios, not 176 independent samples.
3. Let n be retained count and k retained disturbed count. Exact arithmetic oracle:
   eligibility n>=2 and persistence pass when 3*k>=2*n. This yields 2/2, 2/3,
   or 3/4 as the minimum passing counts. Keep n=0 and n=1 as separate rejection
   edge cases; never impute masked observations as quiet observations.
4. Compare each retained result with its full-four-year result. Report pass->fail,
   fail->pass and unchanged totals, grouped by full disturbed count and n.
   Include examples: [1,1,0,0] retaining only its first two years flips fail->pass;
   [1,1,1,0] retaining [1,0,0,1] flips pass->fail.
5. Label retention of quiet/disturbed years explicitly. Uniform enumeration is
   NOT an empirical probability model for cloud-related missingness. Any percent
   is a fraction of enumerated cases, not an estimated field failure probability.
6. Pin boundary semantics from gee/ndvi_change.js: yearly effect is strictly >0.02;
   persistence is >=2/3; recent support is >=2. Hold z and land eligibility true
