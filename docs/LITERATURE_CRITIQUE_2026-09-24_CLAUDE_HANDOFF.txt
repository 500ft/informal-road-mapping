1. CORRECT THE LITERATURE BEFORE USING IT AS A SPECIFICATION

1A. Another method's error rate is not an error floor.
Locations: literature/claim-ledger.md C10; bibliography.md Zhu 2020;
docs/PHASE1_EVALUATION_PACKET.md section 0.
Zhu et al. measured 27% omission and 28% commission for COLD on their Landsat
evaluation [S1]. Those are neither theoretical lower bounds nor predictions for
Mongolian track candidates. Calling them an expected floor could excuse a poor
detector before it is measured. Keep the example with its dataset context.
Replacement: "Other disturbance screens report substantial errors; this screen's
error rates and acceptable review burden have not yet been measured."

1B. Output spacing is not demonstrated minimum detectable road width.
Location: claim-ledger.md C1 and bibliography.md Jia 2023.
Jia et al. produce a 2.5 m output map from 10 m inputs, using learned spatial
relationships and fine-resolution labels [S2]. That statement does not establish
accurate detection of every 2.5 m-wide road, much less a low-contrast Gobi track.
Replace "same sensor, same width, opposite conclusion" with the exact mapping
claim. Keep subpixel detection possible in principle; leave this method's minimum
detectable width unmeasured. Also remove C8's claim that Chemura's scores are an
upper bound: a different dataset and sensor do not mathematically bound ours.

1C. The 30% vegetation statement is being applied to the wrong outcome.
Locations: bibliography.md Okin 2001; topology note's arid-index proposal.
Okin et al.'s simulated hyperspectral unmixing has unreliable vegetation-TYPE
retrieval at low cover, while cover itself can sometimes be estimated reliably
[S3]. It does not prove that all vegetation-index refinements fail below 30% or
that this detector must switch to soil brightness. Remove that universal rule.
Treat soil-adjusted indices as candidates for a controlled comparison.

1D. Recovery remedies are hypotheses, with distinct input requirements.
Locations: claim-ledger.md C11; bibliography.md Ji 2025 / Shi & Xu 2019;
topology note's "sign-stable or sensor-independent channel" proposal.
The Ji study concerns grazing-related vegetation breakpoints; it uses weather
predictors and externally processed InSAR coherence [S4]. It does not validate
road abandonment. Earth Engine's S1_GRD backscatter is not interferometric
coherence [S5]. Scope a coherence data pipeline separately if it becomes useful.
Shi & Xu's paper is explicitly for at-sensor reflectance [S6]; this repository
uses surface reflectance. Require a compatible coefficient source or a tested
transfer before implementation. Brightness is not established here as a
directionally invariant marker of compaction or recovery across substrates.
Segment fitting likewise requires an index, sign convention, sufficient temporal
support, and independently dated changes; naming LandTrendr does not supply them.

1E. A related filter's limitation is not a diagnosis of this implementation.
Locations: claim-ledger.md C12/C14/C15; topology note's first recommendation.
Hannink et al. study Frangi vesselness at vascular crossings [S7]. Our
ridge_strength uses max(0, -lambda_min) times sigma squared; it does not implement
Frangi's eigenvalue-ratio suppression. extract_candidates separately rejects
low-elongation components. The literature motivates looking for filter loss,
but cannot establish that it caused our crossing failure. Inspect stages first.
Also, skeletonizing only ACCEPTED components cannot recover a crossing rejected
before acceptance. Option A must acknowledge that placement conflict.
Do not claim sigma=1..3 means an exclusive detectable width range of 10..30 m:
filter scale is not a hard width cutoff, and ground pixel spacing needs checking.

1F. Recheck the compositing comparison's attribution.
Location: bibliography.md Qiu 2023; gaps.md compositing hazard.
The cited Qiu paper's official abstract says performance depends on interval and
application, with no universally best compositor [S8]. It does not substantiate
the entry's "weighted scoring wins" / "median ~70x cheaper" summary. Locate an
exact source and page for those numbers or remove them pending verification.
Keep Roberts' spectral-consistency concern [S9], but call its magnitude and
downstream effect here unmeasured. A geometric median need not be an actually
observed spectrum either; a medoid selects an observation. "Never observed" alone
is not the test of whether an estimator is useful.

1G. Reduce categorical claims made from incomplete searches.
Location: literature/gaps.md.
Use "not located in this review" instead of "no paper exists" or "empty set".
An unsupported minimum-years rule is an engineering choice requiring validation,
not automatically a novel contribution. Apply the same restraint to OSM coverage
and metric claims. Preserve exact source access levels: metadata, abstract,
methods, or full result; do not award independent-validation strength from a
bibliographic record alone.

Acceptance for this correction task: every changed quantitative claim names its
population, sensor, outcome, metric and access level; linked summaries agree.
These corrections need documentation review, not new software tests.

2. MEASURE THE GRID'S PHYSICAL MEANING BEFORE QUOTING METRES

Observed: gee/ndvi_change.js uses EPSG:3857 at nominal scale 10, bilinear
resampling, a 50-pixel component threshold, and B11 in BSI. B11's native sampling
is 20 m [S10]. Resampling changes the grid; it does not create independent 10 m
SWIR measurements.

Projection concern: Web Mercator's nominal metres differ from ground distances
away from the equator [S11/S12]. Under the spherical approximation, a 10-unit
pixel at 47.3 degrees N is about 6.78 m on the ground and 50 pixels occupy about
2,300 m2, rather than 5,000 m2. These are independently calculated estimates,
not measurements of an exported Earth Engine raster. Exact ellipsoidal geometry,
the delivered affine transform, and neighbourhood-kernel behavior need probing.
Do not assume the metre-based kernel has precisely the same distortion without
testing it. Fractions within an AOI need not suffer the same error as physical
widths, although component selection and smoothing can change with ground scale.

Bounded diagnostic, separate from a gate revision:
1. Use a synthetic, non-square raster and a fixed coordinate at each registered
   latitude. Record projection, affine transform, nominal scale, pixelArea,
   neighbour-centre distances, and the footprint of the metre-based ring kernel.
2. Compare exported corners/centres with an independent geodesic calculation.
   State numerical tolerances before reading results; distinguish approximation
   error from failure. Reuse CAD guidance, not a second coordinate framework.
3. Document B4/B8/B11 alignment and the order of interpolation and index formation.
   Check categorical masks/counts are not inadvertently interpolated as signals.
4. Produce a small table of map units, ground distances, sensor sampling and
   effective support. Never convert path_length_px to metres using an assumed 10.
5. If the intended physical contract differs, propose site-appropriate metric
   grids with pinned origins and a dated migration; do not silently edit the gate.

Acceptance: one independently checked export round-trip, exact transform recorded,
and explicit disposition of the 50-pixel and annulus interpretations. Without
Earth Engine access, finish the specification and label runtime claims untested.

3. TEST COMPOSITING ORDER WITH THE CHEAPEST VALID ALTERNATIVE

Observed: julyS2 computes bandwise medians; julyComposite then derives NDVI/BSI.
These operations generally do not commute. The issue is estimator choice, not
proof that all current values are invalid [S8/S9].

Proposed comparison:
A. Current ratio-of-band-medians baseline, unchanged.
B. Form NDVI, BSI and the per-observation composite from jointly valid bands on
   each acquisition; summarize those indices within July. Explicitly choose
   whether to median the joint composite or combine index medians; they also
   differ. Keep RGB as visualization, not input to the index calculation.
C. Defer medoid/geometric-median unless A/B identifies a decision-relevant issue.

Use a hand-derived counterexample before real imagery:
  red = [0.10, 0.40, 0.30]; NIR = [0.20, 0.50, 0.90].
  NDVI of band medians = (0.50-0.30)/(0.50+0.30) = 0.25.
  Per-acquisition NDVI = [1/3, 1/9, 1/2]; its median = 1/3.
This proves a difference, not which estimator is best. Add one observation with
a missing band to require common validity explicitly.

For any real QA comparison, fix AOIs, dates, masking, grids and samples before
reading predictions. Report differences in signed disturbance, coverage, mask
membership, component fraction and background artifacts, plus runtime. Review
discordant locations blind to which variant produced them. A larger candidate
fraction is not an improvement criterion. Keep all variants as development
evidence; promote only under a dated scientific rationale and independent labels.

4. TURN MISSING-YEAR SUPPORT INTO A MEASURED SENSITIVITY

This is code-derived arithmetic, not a literature-derived optimal threshold:
with four recent years, persistence >=2/3 requires 2/2, 2/3, or 3/4 disturbed
observations depending on which years are valid. The same temporal history can
change decision when missingness preferentially removes quiet or disturbed years.
All recent comparisons also share the early baseline; they are not independent
replications. Current recent-year counts do not summarize early baseline support.

Small experiment:
- Enumerate the 16 binary disturbance histories for four recent years. For each,
  enumerate missingness patterns retaining 2, 3 or 4 years. Derive expected
  persistence decisions by hand/combinatorics; compare with current helpers.
- Distinguish random missingness from deletion of only quiet or disturbed years.
- On fixed QA pixels, record clear observations per July AND contributing early
  and recent years. Scene counts over an AOI do not prove per-pixel usable data.
- After a registered baseline result, use leave-one-year-out diagnostics for early
  and recent windows separately. Report decision flips, not only correlations.

Acceptance: support table and all attempted sensitivity outcomes, with no tuned
minimum-year recommendation masquerading as literature consensus. Keep the
existing >=2 rule unless a separately specified amendment is adopted.

5. ALIGN VALIDATION WITH CONFIRMATION TRIAGE

The current gate measures site-level positive disturbance. Road accuracy, recovery,
network connectivity and regional extent are separate targets. Existing packet
disclaimers are good; the next improvement is a response-design specification
that says which reference observation establishes each claim [S13].

For the first real diagnostic pilot:
- Preserve the four gate sites and holdout restrictions. Do not pick replacement
  sites because the signal looks good. Record failures and missing provenance.
- Use dated, authorized high-resolution evidence to identify corridor presence;
  use temporally appropriate paired evidence for disturbance/recovery attribution.
  A single contemporary image cannot establish historical change or abandonment.
- Fix a small inspection budget and a deterministic candidate selection rule
  before predictions. Report confirmed corridors / reviewed candidates, uncertain
  judgments separately, duplicate reviews, and reviewer minutes per confirmation.
  Treat this as pilot triage yield, not population precision or recall.
- Use separately chosen, fully inspected tiles containing candidates AND areas
  without predictions to estimate missed corridors. Reviewing candidates alone
  cannot estimate recall. Label the sampling design and inclusion probabilities
  before attempting wider-area inference; use an analysis appropriate to that
  design rather than unweighted counts from oversampled candidate strata.
- Record existing confound categories and substrate/land-cover context. Add a
  matched-background diagnostic only prospectively; one distant negative site
  cannot by itself attribute all between-site differences to roads.
- Retain an explicit unknown label and a small blind repeat/adjudication subset.
  Two hours of owner time cannot establish a large reference dataset.

Useful development idea: a DETECTABILITY ENVELOPE rather than one headline score.
After these basics, freeze synthetic sweeps over corridor width, surface contrast,
orientation and subpixel offset. Generate reflectance mixtures first, sample each
band at its native support, and then run the index chain. Add registration shifts
and missing observations independently. Keep hand-defined corridor truth.
Report sensitivity by condition; state that blur/noise assumptions remain models.
Current painted disturbance rasters test geometry, not this sensor-to-signal chain.
This experiment could identify an honest operating range; it cannot establish real
minimum width without imagery-based validation. Defer it if first real evidence
already demonstrates that a different failure dominates.

6. KEEP TOPOLOGY DEFERRED; DIAGNOSE THE EXISTING CROSSING FIRST

If geometry diagnosis is selected, log the existing crossing through these stages:
raw synthetic disturbance -> each-scale ridge response -> quantile mask -> component
membership -> length/elongation rejection -> exported path. Record junction and
arm retention before and after every operation. Compare defaults with ONE named
diagnostic relaxation, keeping the baseline record untouched. This distinguishes
enhancement loss, rejection, and one-path representation loss [S7 + code inspection].

Only propose new filters if enhancement demonstrably loses needed structure.
Only propose branch extraction if accepted components retain that structure and
a confirmation task needs it. The representative corridor path can be sufficient
when the purpose is directing a human to a braided swath. Recovering each rut is
a different target. Do not impose APLS/TOPO merely because literature names them:
network evaluation needs a defined, independently labelled graph output. Before
then, record duplicate candidates and tolerance sensitivity beside current line
scores without claiming those metrics establish topology.

LIMITATIONS AND VERIFICATION
This is a focused audit, not a systematic review of all road-mapping literature.
Publisher access was mixed; S2/S3 were readable through indexed publisher text
although direct opens failed. S6 was metadata-only. No exhaustive non-English or
grey-literature search was performed. A targeted correction/retraction search for
S1-S3 found no relevant notices; it is not a comprehensive Crossmark or retraction
database check. Status of all other references was not exhaustively checked.

Inspected the merged literature, evaluation packet, topology note, GEE script,
extractor and stress-case definitions. The standalone projection arithmetic ran
with Python's math module. A proposed crossing diagnostic could not execute:
the available python3 lacks scipy. No packages were installed and no runtime
crossing result is claimed. No Earth Engine run or imagery judgment was performed.

Evidence strength: strong for the identified wording/transfer errors and the
instrument/projection distinctions; moderate for choosing which diagnostics to
prioritize; insufficient to claim a replacement method will improve real-road
performance. The next useful result is independently verified signal and triage
value, even if it establishes a narrow operating range or a failed screen.
